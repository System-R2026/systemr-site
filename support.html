Support

Practical guidance for the desktop creator workflow.

Account connection issues

Check that the correct creator account is selected, the platform app is configured, and the requested authorization is complete. Never share a token or secret in a support request.

Content upload issues

Confirm that the approved master is readable and that the connected account has the required platform permissions. A transfer workflow is not the same as a public post; review remains with the creator.

Privacy requests

For privacy questions or a request concerning an enabled integration, use the support contact details provided through the official SystemR application or developer listing.

General support

SystemR is currently a desktop creator workflow. Support contact details will be provided through the official SystemR application or developer listing.
