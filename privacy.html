Privacy Policy

A clear summary of the current intended behavior.

The public website

This static website does not create user accounts, use tracking analytics, use advertising cookies, collect payment data, or store social-media credentials. It has no forms, login, database, or backend.

Desktop integrations

SystemR is a desktop creator workflow. When an integration is enabled, credentials and tokens are handled locally or through the relevant platform’s authorization flow. SystemR may process only the information required for that enabled integration.

TikTok integration

The current intended TikTok connection requests user.info.basic to identify the TikTok account authorized by the creator, and video.upload to transfer creator-approved video content to the creator’s TikTok workflow or inbox.

SystemR does not request more information than the enabled workflow requires. SystemR does not publish content without explicit creator approval in the current workflow.

Sharing and retention

SystemR does not sell personal data. The public website does not collect personal data. Any platform data handled by a desktop integration remains subject to the relevant platform’s policies and the creator’s choices.

Questions

Support contact details will be provided through the official SystemR application or developer listing.
